Overview by distance band

Chief Foxall described outside-agency sharing as limited to Texas or roughly 500 miles. Distance is computed from each agency's home state (population-centroid, spot-corrected for large border-state agencies) — a screening tool, not a survey-grade distance. Click a card to filter the agency list below to that band. Data window: Jan 1, 2024 – Aug 13, 2026 (the original export). Federal agencies are shown as their own band; “Unclassified” is only agencies whose name carries no state (e.g. rail police). For Aug–Sep 2026 see the main page, 2026 tabs.
