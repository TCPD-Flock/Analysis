# Reason-field template collapse: findings

Full data: `analysis/data/reason_template_analysis.json` (structured `org`/`network` keys). Scripts: `analysis/scripts/reason_template_*.py`.

**The dropdown theory is confirmed, but the "generic rubber-stamping" fear is not borne out by the evidence.** Both logs flip over in **January 2026** — cleanly in the Org log (one department, one system: 62.7% of that month's search volume used reason strings never seen in any prior month), more gradually in the Network log (5,363 independently-run agencies transitioning to the new Flock UI on their own schedules, but the actual content flips completely between December 2025 and January 2026 regardless). Post-cutover text does come from a real closed taxonomy — 48 fixed primary categories for Colony's own officers (top 30 cover 98.5% of volume) and roughly 1,715 primary categories network-wide (top 30 still cover 94.6%, since each of the 5,363 agencies appears to configure its own category list rather than sharing one global set).

But the data does **not** support "officers/agencies are just clicking a generic default instead of justifying searches": concentration in the single most-common value actually *fell* after the change, in both logs (Org: 16.2%→10.3%; Network: 7.2%→4.3%) — the opposite of what pure default-clicking would produce. Zero of the top 100 agencies and only 9 of 104 officers hit the ≥90%-single-value threshold, and 8 of those 9 officers have post-cutover volume of 10 searches or fewer (small-sample noise, not a pattern) — the one real near-miss is **Coweta County GA SO** at 88.4% on a single value (`"other - 37"`). Roughly 41% of Org-log rows and 63% of Network-log rows still carry a genuine free-text suffix appended to the category (case numbers, department-specific phrasing) — the narrative detail didn't disappear, it moved into a structured `category - detail` format. What *did* shrink sharply is raw vocabulary size for high-volume users: Houston TX PD alone went from 9,203 distinct reason strings pre-cutover to 610 post (Dallas 12,543→513) — a real narrowing, just not a collapse to one bucket.
